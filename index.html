Canberra Theatre

Location Civic
Parking Accessible permit parking available in the City Hall and Hobart Place car parks. Limited nearby parking with the lightrail construction. The closest accessible drop-off and pickup location is via Constitution Avenue, in the Lyric Lane turning circle.
Bathrooms tbc
Wheelchair Access Some wheelchair seats available
Seating tbc
Foyer Some seating available in foyer.
Accessible Accommodations Offers audio described performances but not very often and doesn't seem to have an option to request them on the website.

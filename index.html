The Street Theatre

Location Civic
Parking Parking near the main entrance to the complex provides a set-down and pick-up point for those with specific access requirements. This includes two dedicated accessible parking spaces. Some street parking available along Childers St and University Ave. Can be hard to find a parking space nearby, so allow extra time for parking.
Bathrooms There are two dedicated accessible toilets within the complex.
Wheelchair access 2 wheelchair seats available in Street One, with floor level seating in row A. They offer the options to either transfer from wheelchair to seat, or to clear a space for your chair. They can also reserve space next to you for any companions or friends attending the performance. To do so, call the box office on 6247 1223 to book.
Street Two and Street Three can accommodate any patrons in wheelchairs or with mobility concerns with their seating layouts.
Seating Seating in Street One is allocated and tiered. The first row is accessible without stairs. Seating in Street Two and Three is non-reserved, but seats can be reserved at the front or on the ends case by case for accessibility needs, just contact the box office.
Foyer Some seating available in the foyer. Foyer can get crowded for busy shows but there is also space outside the venue where you can still pay attention to when the doors open.
Hearing accommodations “LISTEN Everywhere” wifi based assisted hearing system available for all performances in Street One. Can connect to your own device, headphones, or neck-loop, or the provided iPods. You can email foh@thestreet.org.au for more info.
While it is not available outside Street One, Street Two and Street Three are both smaller venues and you can request seating near the front.
Sight accommodations Staff can assist with navigating the space, especially the stairs in Street One. Seats can also be reserved at the front in any space.
Other accommodations Companion cards (must book on the phone or in person)
Shows that are highly visual, with little or no spoken word, will be indicated online.
All seats in Street One have clear sightlines to the stage.
No restrictions on assistance animals.
Children aged 2 or under are allowed free entry to all performances, provided the guardian will have the child seated on their lap.
Performer access Street One stage is wheelchair accessible through the back of the venue via the loading dock, however the backstage area is accessed via shallow steps. There is no accessible bathroom backstage.
The Street Two performance space is fully accessible.
The Street Three performance space is fully accessible, the backstage is accessible via ramp, and there is an accessible bathroom and shower backstage.
Notes Street Three was built more recently and as such is a lot more accessible, although it is a smaller space and mostly used for rehearsals.
If you have other accessibility requests, contact the box office, they work very hard to make sure The Street is accessible for everyone.

Belco Arts Centre

Location Belconnen
Parking 4 two-hour accessible parking spaces are available in the Belco Arts car park. There is limited parking in the Belco Arts car park, more parking is available across the road or at the Westfield, a short walk away.
Bathrooms There are multiple accessible toilets across the Centre; next to the Dance Studio, near the Workshops, and next to The Theatre.
Wheelchair access The entire Centre is on a single level and is wheelchair and mobility aid accessible. When booking for a theatre show with a wheelchair or scooter, they ask that you contact them. A wheelchair is also available for use by visitors; please talk to staff at Reception or Box Office if you wish to use this.
Seating There is a pop-up accessibility requirements box in the online booking system to request specific seating for unallocated seating performances. Seating in the Theatre is tiered on steps. The front row can be accessed without steps.
Foyer Limited seating available in the foyer. For booked out shows, the foyer might get crowded before doors open and in intermission.
Hearing accommodations Belconnen Arts Centre has hearing loops in the Generator and Pivot Galleries, The Theatre, Theatre Foyer, and Rehearsal Room. In the Generator and Pivot Galleries, Theatre Foyer and Rehearsal Room, please switch your device to T to receive audio. To receive audio in The Theatre, please request a pendant device from Box Office and switch it to T.
Sight accommodations Some performances may provide specific accommodations, see listing on the website for more details.
Other accommodations Assistance animals welcome in all areas. Water bowls available on request.
Companion cards accepted (must book on the phone or in person)
Performer access Backstage areas are wheelchair accessible. The stage in the Theatre is modular and can be configured to have a ramp. There is an accessible toilet, shower dressing rooms, and green rooms backstage, all wheelchair accessible.

Performing Arts Venues in the Canberra Region

Information has been sourced from the websites linked with each venue, as well as contact with the venues.
Wheelchair accessibility will be checked with community who use mobility aids to make sure it is accurate, and will be updated to reflect their experience.

Canberra Theatre Centre

Location Civic
Parking Accessible permit parking available in the City Hall and Hobart Place car parks. Limited nearby parking with the light rail construction. The closest accessible drop-off and pickup location is via Constitution Avenue, in the Lyric Lane turning circle.
More parking info here
Bathrooms tbc
Wheelchair access Some wheelchair seats available when booking on the phone or in person.
Seating Special seating requests available when booking on the phone or in person.
Foyer Some seating available in foyer.
Hearing accommodations Auslan interpreted for select performances.
Live captioning for select performances.
FM radio assistance hearing units for all performances.
Sight accommodations Audio described for select performances.
Tactile tours available.
Other accommodations Relaxed performances for select performances
Babes in arms performances for select performances
Mobility assistance
Companion cards accepted when booking on the phone or in person
Notes

The Q

Location Queanbeyan
Parking 8 accessible parking spaces in the Lowe Street car park, directly outside the entrance to the Q. 3 accessible parking spaces in the underground carpark accessed via Rutledge Street (with a lift to the entrance), and 2 accessible parking spaces on Crawford Street near the entrance to The B.
Bathrooms tbc
Wheelchair access Ramp access to venue from the front entrance. 2 wheelchair seats available in the theatre.
Seating Aisle seating can be requested when booking.
Foyer Some seating available in foyer.
Accessibility options Hearing assistance
Special seating requests
Companion cards
Notes Wheelchair and special seating options, mobility assistance, and companion card tickets require booking in person or over the phone.
Many other options only available on select performances

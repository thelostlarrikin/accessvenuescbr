Performing Arts Venues in the Canberra Region

Information has been sourced from the websites linked with each venue, as well as contact with the venues.
Some information is based on personal experience of people living with disabilities accessing these venues, particularly information about navigating the space.
Wheelchair accessibility will be checked with community who use mobility aids to make sure it is accurate, and will be updated to reflect their experience. This is an ongoing process, so some venues may not have updated information yet.
Have more info about a venue? See something that needs to be updated? Have a venue you want added to the database? Send an email to accessvenuescbr@gmail.com

The Street Theatre

Location Civic
Parking Parking near the main entrance to the complex provides a set-down and pick-up point for those with specific access requirements. This includes two dedicated accessible parking spaces. Some street parking available along Childers St and University Ave. Can be hard to find a parking space nearby, so allow extra time for parking.
Bathrooms There are two dedicated accessible toilets within the complex.
Wheelchair access 2 wheelchair seats available in Street One, with floor level seating in row A. They offer the options to either transfer from wheelchair to seat, or to clear a space for your chair. They can also reserve space next to you for any companions or friends attending the performance. To do so, call the box office on 6247 1223 to book.
Street Two and Street Three can accommodate any patrons in wheelchairs or with mobility concerns with their seating layouts.
Seating Seating in Street One is allocated and tiered. The first row is accessible without stairs. Seating in Street Two and Three is non-reserved, but seats can be reserved at the front or on the ends case by case for accessibility needs, just contact the box office.
Foyer Some seating available in the foyer. Foyer can get crowded for busy shows but there is also space outside the venue where you can still pay attention to when the doors open.
Hearing accommodations “LISTEN Everywhere” wifi based assisted hearing system available for all performances in Street One. Can connect to your own device, headphones, or neck-loop, or the provided iPods. You can email foh@thestreet.org.au for more info.
While it is not available outside Street One, Street Two and Street Three are both smaller venues and you can request seating near the front.
Sight accommodations Staff can assist with navigating the space, especially the stairs in Street One. Seats can also be reserved at the front in any space.
Other accommodations Companion cards (must book on the phone or in person)
Shows that are highly visual, with little or no spoken word, will be indicated online.
All seats in Street One have clear sightlines to the stage.
No restrictions on assistance animals.
Children aged 2 or under are allowed free entry to all performances, provided the guardian will have the child seated on their lap.
Performer access Street One stage is wheelchair accessible through the back of the venue via the loading dock, however the backstage area is accessed via shallow steps. There is no accessible bathroom backstage.
The Street Two performance space is fully accessible.
The Street Three performance space is fully accessible, the backstage is accessible via ramp, and there is an accessible bathroom and shower backstage.
Notes Street Three was built more recently and as such is a lot more accessible, although it is a smaller space and mostly used for rehearsals.
If you have other accessibility requests, contact the box office, they work very hard to make sure The Street is accessible for everyone.

Belco Arts Centre

Location Belconnen
Parking 4 two-hour accessible parking spaces are available in the Belco Arts car park. There is limited parking in the Belco Arts car park, more parking is available across the road or at the Westfield, a short walk away.
Bathrooms There are multiple accessible toilets across the Centre; next to the Dance Studio, near the Workshops, and next to The Theatre.
Wheelchair access The entire Centre is on a single level and is wheelchair and mobility aid accessible. When booking for a theatre show with a wheelchair or scooter, they ask that you contact them. A wheelchair is also available for use by visitors; please talk to staff at Reception or Box Office if you wish to use this.
Seating There is a pop-up accessibility requirements box in the online booking system to request specific seating for unallocated seating performances. Seating in the Theatre is tiered on steps. The front row can be accessed without steps.
Foyer Limited seating available in the foyer. For booked out shows, the foyer might get crowded before doors open and in intermission.
Hearing accommodations Belconnen Arts Centre has hearing loops in the Generator and Pivot Galleries, The Theatre, Theatre Foyer, and Rehearsal Room. In the Generator and Pivot Galleries, Theatre Foyer and Rehearsal Room, please switch your device to T to receive audio. To receive audio in The Theatre, please request a pendant device from Box Office and switch it to T.
Sight accommodations Some performances may provide specific accommodations, see listing on the website for more details.
Other accommodations Assistance animals welcome in all areas. Water bowls available on request.
Companion cards accepted (must book on the phone or in person)
Performer access Backstage areas are wheelchair accessible. The stage in the Theatre is modular and can be configured to have a ramp. There is an accessible toilet, shower dressing rooms, and green rooms backstage, all wheelchair accessible.

Tuggeranong Arts Centre

Location Tuggeranong
Parking There is limited parking at the Tuggeranong Art Centre. There is one accessible parking space in the front carpark. If you or someone in your party has mobility issues where access to their carpark is essential, they say to contact the Centre on 6293 1443 and they will reserve a space for you.
Other nearby parking is a 1 minute walk to the centre but is paid parking.
Bathrooms TAC has left-hand transfer accessible unisex toilets located upstairs and downstairs. Gender-specific bathrooms are also available on both floors.
Wheelchair access There is a ramp from the front carpark to the main entrance. The lift to the first floor is located just inside the main entrance, on the lefthand side and opposite the stairs. The lift dimensions are: 2100mm (h) x 1450mm (d). The door is 880mm (w). Wheelchair seating is available in the back row of the theatre and can be booked when purchasing tickets online, or at reception.
Seating If you are sitting towards the front, you may wish to ask a staff member to escort you through the downstairs entrance, due to the steeply-raked seating.
Foyer The foyer is small with very limited seating available.
Hearing accommodations There is a hearing loop and infrared hearing augmentation system installed in the Theatre for patrons. It is available for all performances- unless in the specific instance of an in-house performance presented entirely acoustically without amplification.
Sight accommodations The Front of House team is available to provide assistance and guidance throughout the venue for patrons who are blind or have low vision. Staff can also offer verbal descriptions of artworks displayed within the gallery spaces upon request. In addition, the Centre features high-visibility yellow handrails that assist visitors in locating and navigating to the main entrance.
Other accommodations Assistance animals welcome in all areas.
Companion Cards accepted (must book in person or online).
Support workers can accompany their clients in all classes and workshops but cannot join in as a participant without paying a discounted course fee, which is at the discretion of the CEO.
Performer access They work collaboratively with performers and their representatives to accommodate individual access needs, including travel, accommodation, and venue-related requirements wherever possible.
All backstage areas are wheelchair accessible and connected by wide, unobstructed, and well-lit pathways. Accesible light switches are also incorporated throughout the route between backstage areas and the Theatre.
The stage can be accessed by wheelchair from all three stage wings.
The greenroom is wheelchair accessible. The accessible toilet in the foyer is directly adjacent to the backstage area.

ACT Hub

Location Kingston
Parking There is a small dirt/gravel carpark, but because ACT Hub is heritage listed they can't mark accessible bays. If you let them know in advance they're happy to reserve spaces with cones.
Bathrooms Has an accessible bathroom
Wheelchair access There is ramp access into the foyers for patrons, as well as ramp access to the kitchen area. Seating can be reserved if you contact them ahead of time.
Seating Their seating is usually unallocated, but if you contact them with requirments they can reserve seats for you.
Foyer There is limited seating available in the foyer.
Hearing accommodations They currently do not offer specific services but the venue is small.
Sight accommodations They currently do not offer specific services but guide dogs and service dogs are welcome.
Other accommodations Companion cards accepted (phone or email to book enquiries@acthub.com.au ).
Service animals welcome.
For other requirements, contact them when booking and they'll see what they can do to meet your access requirements.
Performer access The staging for each production at ACT Hub is different, so they can modify the stage and backstage areas to suit the access requirements of the performers. There is a ramp to the dressing rooms from outside, and the accessible bathroom is available to performers. Heritage restrictions mean the ground beyond the external ramp is grass, but it is kept flat and mowed.

Mill Theatre at Dairy Road

Location Fyshwick
Parking Free parking is available within the presinct, with 2 accessible parking spaces near the theatre.
Bathrooms Accessible bathrooms available within Building 3.3, along the hallway outside the theatre.
Wheelchair access Ramp access to the theatre. Fully accessible venue. Designated accessible seating available, contact venue before booking at hello@mill
Seating They encourage patrons to contact them before booking if you would like to request specific seating options. This includes requests in areas with reduced sensory impact, easier access, or a particular viewing position. Patrons with accessibility requirements will be offered early access to the theatre upon arrival, allowing them to enter and settle into their allocated seats before general audience admission begins.
Foyer
Hearing accommodations The Mill does not currently have a hearing loop or hearing augmentation system permanently installed. These services would need to be arranged through their technical provider, Side Stage.
The theatre has an existing sound system, including speakers and audio mixing equipment.
Where available, productions may provide written information, captions or surtitles. These services vary between productions and are not guaranteed.
Sight accommodations The Mill does not currently provide a formal audio description service. These services would need to be arranged through their technical provider, Side Stage.
Tactile offerings and other access services can be arranged on request, depending on the production. The theatre team can also provide information about the visual elements of individual productions, including lighting effects, projections, and significant stage action.
Other accommodations Guide dogs and assistance animals are welcome.
Companion cards accepted.
Performer access The stage is wheelchair accessible. THe backstage areas are accessible for performers but not crew. There is no greenroom space in the venue, the theatre is very small. The only bathrooms are those for the general public, outside the theatre (but in the building) which includes an accessible bathroom.
Notes The Mill is a small, intimate theatre. This creates a particularly close relationship between performers and audiences, but it can also mean that the intensity of a performance is experienced at close range. Productions may include loud sound effects, changes in lighting, darkness, flashing lights, projections and emotionally intense material.

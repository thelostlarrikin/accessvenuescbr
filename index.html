Canberra Theatre Centre

Location Civic
Parking Accessible permit parking available in the City Hall and Hobart Place car parks. Limited nearby parking with the light rail construction. The closest accessible drop-off and pickup location is via Constitution Avenue, in the Lyric Lane turning circle.

The Q

Location Queanbeyan
Parking 8 accessible parking spaces in the Lowe Street car park, directly outside the entrance to the Q. 3 accessible parking spaces in the underground carpark accessed via Rutledge Street (with a lift to the entrance), and 2 accessible parking spaces on Crawford Street near the entrance to The B.
Bathrooms tbc
Wheelchair access Ramp access to venue from the front entrance. 2 wheelchair seats available in the theatre.
Seating Aisle seating can be requested when booking.
Foyer Some seating available in foyer.
